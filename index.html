QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

How do I enroll in a course?

Open the course catalogue, choose a course and press Buy. Your order details open in WhatsApp so we can confirm enrollment with you.

Are the prices final?

Prices on the site are shown in Bangladeshi taka (BDT) as examples. Please confirm the final fee with us before enrolling.

Is payment taken on this website?

No. No payment is taken on this website. Enrollment and payment details are handled with you over WhatsApp.

Do I need an account?

You can browse every course without one. A free account (email or Google) gives you a personal profile.

I have a coupon code. Where do I use it?

Press Buy on any course, then type your code in the “Have a coupon?” box to see the updated price.
